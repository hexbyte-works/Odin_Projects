Ingredients needed to make lasagna

  • meat or vegetable
  • onion
  • garlic
  • tomato paste
  • olive oil
  • salt & pepper
  • herbs
  • cheese
  • butter
  • flour

Steps to make lasagna

  1. Prepare the sauce: Sauté onion and garlic, add minced meat, then add tomato sauce, paste, herbs, salt, and pepper. Simmer.
  2. Make béchamel: Melt butter, add flour, then gradually whisk in milk until thick.
  3. Layer: In a baking dish, add meat sauce → lasagna sheets → béchamel → cheese. Repeat.
  4. Top: Finish with béchamel and plenty of cheese.
  5. Bake: Bake at 180°C (350°F) for about 35–45 minutes.
  6. Rest: Let it cool for 10–15 minutes, then slice and serve.
